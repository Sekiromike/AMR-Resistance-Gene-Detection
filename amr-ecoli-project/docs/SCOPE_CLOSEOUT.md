  human-clinical development isolates and 1,505 external Japanese isolates.

  AMRFinderPlus allele/family features, and selected resistance-locus sequences.
- Frozen Evo 2 embeddings: 64 shards and their original checksums.

  variant-withholding comparisons. All 54 archived evaluations, 438 paired
  comparisons, 192 source-exclusion summaries, and the withholding read-out
  reproduced from the completed predictions. The 18 prediction locks match.
- Primary withholding denominator: 568 target–isolate observations, 565 unique
  isolates, 45 lineages. Above-threshold shares are descriptive MIC read-outs.
